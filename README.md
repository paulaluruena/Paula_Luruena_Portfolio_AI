# Paula Luruena Marquez — Psychology for the people who use AI

A portfolio for **user research, consumer insights, and societal-impact research roles at companies building AI**. It uses all of Paula's background (research lab, applied behavior work, clinical observation, crisis support, bilingual community work, and business roles) and gives the emphasis to how people use, trust, and are changed by AI.

Website: https://paulaluruena.github.io/Paula_Luruena_Portfolio_AIxPeople/

## What is on it

- **Opening:** one statement of what she wants to study, a short introduction, and key facts.
- **How I read a conversation:** a short exchange between a person and an AI assistant, coded with a five-code codebook. Choosing a code highlights where it appears and shows the follow-up question she would ask. Visitors can also code the exchange themselves and compare their coding with hers. The exchange is illustrative and labeled as such; it is not participant data.
- **Three studies I would like to run:** research proposals in the format a research team uses (question, why it matters, method, measures, the decision it informs, limits). They are labeled as proposals, not completed studies.
- **What I have built and studied with AI:** Split It, SpeakEasy, the staffing and recruiting project, and the individual "AI and me" analysis.
- **Experience:** every role, each with a margin note on what it prepared her to do in research on people and AI.
- **Background:** education, languages, tools, community, and her other interests.
- **Why psychology (`story.html`):** the origin story from her other portfolios, with a final part written for this focus.

Professional facts come from Paula's résumé. Clinical observation is described as observational, and skills in development are labeled that way. Street address and phone number are left off.

## Files

- `index.html` — portfolio page
- `story.html` — "Why psychology"
- `styles.css` — design: a relative of the other portfolios (Bodoni Moda and Hanken Grotesk, the same paper, ink and wine, a side rail with live clocks), plus the five highlighter colors of the codebook
- `script.js` — codebook highlighting, the code-it-yourself comparison, proposal tabs, live clocks, section tracking in the top bar, copy-email button, reading progress
- `assets/` — portrait, résumé, and local fonts with their licenses

No build step. Open `index.html` in a browser, or publish from **main → / (root)** with GitHub Pages. The content stays readable without JavaScript, and motion is reduced for people who ask for that.

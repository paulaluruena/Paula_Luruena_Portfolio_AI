# Paula Luruena Marquez — Psychology for the people who use AI

A portfolio for **user research, consumer insights, and societal-impact research roles at companies building AI**. It uses all of Paula's background (research lab, applied behavior work, clinical observation, crisis support, bilingual community work, and business roles) and gives the emphasis to how people use, trust, and are changed by AI.

Website: https://paulaluruena.github.io/Paula_Luruena_Portfolio_AI/

## What is on it

In this order:

- **Opening:** one statement of what she wants to study, a short introduction, and key facts.
- **Background:** education, languages, tools, community, and her other interests.
- **Experience:** every role, each with a note on what it prepared her to do in research on people and AI.
- **Built with AI:** a case study of Split It (how it works, and how it is designed for a model that can be wrong) with a working preview of the app in a phone frame, followed by SpeakEasy, the staffing and recruiting project, and the "AI and me" analysis. The Split It description is taken from the app's own code in `paulaluruena/Split-it`.
- **How I read a conversation:** one short exchange between a person and an AI assistant, analyzed in five steps: read, code against a written codebook, interpret across the codes, turn each finding into something testable, and a "your turn" step where visitors code it themselves and compare, with the reasoning for every sentence. The exchange is illustrative and labeled as such; it is not participant data.
- **Three studies I would like to run:** research proposals in one compact viewer. They are labeled as proposals, not completed studies. The three are: whether answers people like in the moment are the ones they value a week later, whether help that fades builds more independent skill, and what makes a referral to human help work.
- **Why psychology (`story.html`):** the origin story from her other portfolios, with a final part written for this focus.

Professional facts come from Paula's résumé. Clinical observation is described as observational, and skills in development are labeled that way. Street address and phone number are left off.

## Files

- `index.html` — portfolio page
- `story.html` — "Why psychology"
- `styles.css` — design: a relative of the other portfolios (Bodoni Moda and Hanken Grotesk, the same paper, ink and wine, a side rail with live clocks), plus the five highlighter colors of the codebook
- `script.js` — the Split It preview, the five-step conversation analysis, the study viewer, live clocks, section tracking, copy-email button, reading progress
- `assets/` — portrait, résumé, and local fonts with their licenses

No build step. Open `index.html` in a browser, or publish from **main → / (root)** with GitHub Pages. The content stays readable without JavaScript, and motion is reduced for people who ask for that.
